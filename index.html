🧭 What is AirPoise?

AirPoise is a free, open-source Mac app that lives quietly in your menu bar. It uses the built-in motion sensors in your AirPods Pro (or AirPods 3) to detect your head position — no camera, no cloud, no privacy concerns.

Think of it as two powerful tools in one:

  • 🪑 Posture Coach: It gently reminds you when you slouch, helping you build healthier sitting habits throughout the day.
  • ⚡ Gesture Shortcuts: Nod, shake your head, or tilt to trigger custom actions on your Mac — like pausing music, switching apps, or muting your mic.

Everything runs 100% on your device. Your head-tracking data never leaves your Mac.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Absolutely. AirPoise is completely on-device. No data is sent to any server, and there's no cloud component whatsoever. Your head-tracking information stays between you and your Mac.

Which AirPods work with AirPoise?

AirPoise uses Apple's Core Motion framework, which is available on AirPods Pro (1st and 2nd generation) and AirPods 3. These models have the necessary motion sensors built in.

Does AirPoise drain my AirPods battery?

AirPoise relies on the same head-tracking sensors used by Apple's Spatial Audio feature, so the battery impact is minimal and comparable to normal AirPods usage.
